# Prompts das fotografias-base

As duas imagens foram geradas com o modo integrado do `imagegen`. Texto, marca, interface e CTA foram adicionados posteriormente no HTML; não foram renderizados pelo modelo de imagem.

## Clínica de estética

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: background photograph for a premium 4:5 Instagram ad aimed at owners of Brazilian aesthetic clinics.
Primary request: Create a candid documentary photograph inside a modern Brazilian facial aesthetics clinic. A Brazilian female aesthetic professional in her mid-30s, dark hair tied back, wearing an elegant dark forest-green clinical uniform, is fully focused on caring for an adult Brazilian female client lying comfortably on a treatment bed during a gentle facial cleansing session. The professional's posture and hands must look anatomically natural and competent. A plain matte-black smartphone sits visibly on a small side counter in the middle background, screen softly lit as if receiving a message, but with no readable content.
Style/medium: premium editorial healthcare photography, photorealistic, authentic skin texture, natural fabric folds, no stock-photo posing, no artificial glamour retouching.
Scene/backdrop: clean contemporary aesthetics clinic in Manaus, subtle warm wood, cream surfaces, sparse green details, realistic equipment, uncluttered.
Color: deep forest green, warm cream, muted natural skin tones, a tiny restrained guarana-red accent in an object only; soft warm daylight from a window mixed with practical room light.
Composition/framing: vertical portrait composition suitable for cropping to 1080x1350, eye-level medium-wide shot, 35mm lens, professional and client occupy the center-right, generous darker negative space in the upper-left and lower area for later graphic copy, smartphone visible but secondary, shallow-to-medium depth of field.
Atmosphere: calm, attentive, premium but approachable; the viewer should feel that the professional is present with the client while another conversation waits quietly in the background.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand marks, no watermark, no floating notifications, no holograms, no extra limbs or fingers, no medical drama, no needles, no before-and-after framing, no exaggerated beauty result.
```

## Clínica de fisioterapia

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: background photograph for a premium 4:5 Instagram ad aimed at owners of Brazilian physiotherapy clinics.
Primary request: Create a candid documentary photograph inside a modern Brazilian physiotherapy clinic. A Brazilian male physiotherapist in his late 30s, medium-brown skin, short dark hair, wearing a refined deep forest-green polo and neutral clinical trousers, is fully focused on guiding an adult Brazilian female client through a gentle shoulder mobility exercise. His posture, hands and the client's body alignment must look anatomically natural and professionally credible. A plain matte-black smartphone rests visibly on a nearby bench in the middle background, screen softly lit as if receiving a message, but with no readable content.
Style/medium: premium editorial healthcare photography, photorealistic, authentic skin texture and fabric, candid not posed, no glossy stock-photo look.
Scene/backdrop: bright contemporary physiotherapy studio in Manaus, cream walls, warm wood details, a resistance band and treatment table used naturally, sparse green accents, uncluttered and believable.
Color: forest green, warm cream, natural skin tones and soft wood; restrained warm daylight with gentle practical lighting.
Composition/framing: vertical portrait composition suitable for cropping to 1080x1350, eye-level medium-wide shot, 35mm lens, therapist and client occupy center-right, generous darker negative space in the upper-left and lower area for later graphic copy, smartphone visible but secondary, shallow-to-medium depth of field.
Atmosphere: calm, human, competent and reassuring; the viewer should feel that the therapist is completely present with the client while another conversation waits quietly in the background.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand marks, no watermark, no floating notifications, no holograms, no extra limbs or fingers, no pain grimace, no medical emergency, no before-and-after framing.
```
