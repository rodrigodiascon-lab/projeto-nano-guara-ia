# Produção visual

## Modo

Geração de imagem nova com a ferramenta integrada de imagens, usada como fotografia-base do criativo `nutricionista-01`. Interfaces, textos e destaques do celular foram compostos em HTML/CSS para preservar legibilidade e reutilização.

## Prompt exato da fotografia-base

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: vertical 4:5 advertising background for a premium Brazilian AI assistant campaign aimed at nutritionists. Primary request: Create a trustworthy consultation scene with a Brazilian female nutritionist around 35 years old, medium-brown skin and dark hair neatly tied back, wearing an elegant forest-green blouse with a cream professional coat, speaking calmly with an adult female client across a modern consultation desk. This is an initial conversation, not a medical procedure and not a body-transformation scene. Scene/backdrop: contemporary nutrition office with cream walls, dark-green accents, subtle warm wood, a small restrained bowl of fresh fruit and a closed neutral notebook as environmental cues. Composition/framing: vertical portrait. The nutritionist is on the left-middle and the client is seen naturally from behind or three-quarter view on the right. Place a matte-black smartphone clearly visible on the front-left corner of the desk in the lower-left area. Its screen has a soft generic green confirmation glow with no readable interface. Leave usable negative space in the upper-right and right side for ad copy. Style/medium: premium natural commercial editorial photography, realistic skin, hands and anatomy, restrained depth of field. Lighting/mood: soft daylight, welcoming, calm and credible. Color palette: forest green, deep green, cream and warm wood. Constraints: no text, no logo, no watermark, no price, no scale, no measuring tape, no before-and-after comparison, no weight-loss implication, no clinical result, no diagnosis, no medical charts, no exaggerated smiles. Keep the phone noticeable but naturally integrated into the scene.
