# Produção visual

## Modo

Geração de imagem nova, usada como fotografia-base do criativo `dentista-01`. As interfaces, os textos e os destaques do celular foram compostos em HTML/CSS para preservar legibilidade e permitir reutilização.

## Prompt exato da fotografia-base

> Create a photorealistic natural advertising background for a premium Brazilian AI assistant campaign aimed at dental clinics. Vertical 4:5 composition. A Brazilian female dentist around 35 years old, medium-brown skin, dark hair neatly tied back, wearing elegant forest-green dental scrubs and clear protective glasses, is speaking calmly with an adult patient seated in a modern dental chair. Show a consultation moment before or after an appointment, not an invasive procedure: no close-up of the mouth, no instrument inside the mouth, no needles, no surgery. The clinic is clean and contemporary with cream walls, dark green accents and subtle warm wood. Place a matte black smartphone visibly on a nearby side tray in the lower-left to middle-left area; its screen is softly lit with a generic green confirmation glow but contains no readable text. Make the phone easy to notice while still feeling naturally part of the scene. Leave generous clean negative space on the upper-right and right side for headline and ad copy. Soft natural daylight, premium editorial commercial photography, realistic skin and hands, restrained depth of field, trustworthy and welcoming mood. No text, no logo, no watermark, no price, no before-and-after comparison, no exaggerated smiles or clinical-result implication.
